# Hilbert–Pólya approach to the Riemann theorem

Pierre-Luc Dallaire-Demers uses Alpöge’s critical-line theorem to construct a self-adjoint Hamiltonian whose positive energies are the positive ordinates of the nontrivial zeta zeros. Section 3 estimates the slope jump at $\log 2$ from three samples of the Ramsey coherence. The error bound accounts for the finite energy cutoff and the precision of each measurement. [Paper, pp. 5–7](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pauli-Group/Riemann/3cdeb2f09880b11532de384d0bca5c2c74ad88f9/hilbert-polya-riemann-quantum-physicists.pdf#page=5)

For the 547/727 theta race, Lee Crossley recovers the slope jump at $\log(p^j)$ exactly from three weighted samples of the explicit-formula function after removing its Archimedean term. At an odd prime, two such jumps and the local degree give the Euler factor of the Dirichlet character product. The identity for the explicit-formula function is unconditional. Its interpretation in terms of the limiting prime race assumes Dirichlet GRH for the primitive factors of the race and its base field, including the Riemann zeta function.
