# Hilbert–Pólya approach to the Riemann theorem

Pierre-Luc Dallaire-Demers estimates the slope jump at $\log 2$ from three samples of the Ramsey coherence. [Paper, section 3, p. 6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Pauli-Group/Riemann/d6f5fb4abb1dd57091a3ebf570cf510e9140c76e/hilbert-polya-riemann-quantum-physicists.pdf#page=6).

For a related prime-race observable, Lee Crossley recovers the slope jump at $\log(p^j)$ exactly from three weighted samples after subtracting the smooth contribution. For odd primes, two such jumps and the known local degree give the local Euler factor of the Dirichlet character product studied there. [Sampling identity](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-curvature-euler-factors/blob/f73465f076e94b4029fa3f3095df9b9f14a066b4/PROOF.md#L60-L76) and [paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24412642).
