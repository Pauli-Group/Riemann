# Fourth-power samples

At $t=4\log k$, the smoothed prime sum $E_\Lambda(t)$ in equation (3.3) is exactly the centred ramp $G(k^4)$ in Crossley’s Theorem 11.1. The factor $e^{-t/2}/Z_*$ in the readout becomes $1/(k^2 Z_*)$. [Dallaire-Demers, §3](./hilbert-polya-riemann-quantum-physicists.pdf#page=9).

Theorem 11.1 characterises RH by how often this arithmetic input is large. For each $1/2<\theta<1$, there must be constants $c\ge0$ and $\varepsilon>0$ such that, for arbitrarily large integers $L$, at most $L^{3-\varepsilon}$ integers $k$ in $[L^2,L^3]$ satisfy $E_\Lambda(4\log k)>c k^{4\theta}$. [Crossley, Theorem 11.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24466908).
